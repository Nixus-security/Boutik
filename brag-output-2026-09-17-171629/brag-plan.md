# Brag Plan: Boutik — Import Excel & Relance WhatsApp

## What is this app?
Boutik is a stock + orders tool for small shop owners who sell on WhatsApp. This video explains two specific features: bulk-importing stock from an Excel file, and one-tap WhatsApp payment reminders for unpaid clients.

## The angle
A quick two-step tutorial, not a hype reel: "here's exactly how these two things work." Real screens, real copy, confident pacing — the kind of clip you'd send a new shop owner to explain the app in 20 seconds.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Text over brand green: "2 trucs qui font gagner du temps." Sets up "two things" before diving into the first.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Import page: tap to choose an Excel file, the parsed preview table appears with rows (name / price / stock / category), one row flagged for correction, then "Enregistrer" confirms and the products land in the real list.
- A short bridge card names the second feature: "Relancer un client qui n'a pas payé."
- Relances page: an overdue client card ("Aïcha Koné — impayé depuis 6 jours"), tap "Relancer", the exact pre-filled WhatsApp message appears in a chat-bubble reveal.

## Outro / punchline
Logo lockup + line tying both features together: "Importe. Vends. Relance. Tout depuis ton téléphone."

## User flow worth showing
1. Import: pick Excel file → corrections table → save → real product list.
2. Relance: list of unpaid clients → tap Relancer → pre-filled WhatsApp message ready to send.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: two-step tutorial, clean and confident, no jokes — feature-card clarity
- Interpretation: smooth reveals, short readable labels naming each step, restrained motion — the pacing should feel like a product demo, not a launch hype reel.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: 20s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#f9fafb` (app gray-50) for UI scenes; brand green (`#127847` → `#124e32`) for label/bridge and outro cards
- Accent: `#1cb96c` (brand-500), deep accent `#127847` (brand-700)
- Text: `#111827` on light scenes, white on green scenes
- Display/body font: system-ui, bold weights (matches app's rendered look)
- Strongest visual element: the Excel-preview table turning into a confirmed product list; the WhatsApp chat-bubble reveal of the real relance message text

## Share copy (draft)
Importe ton stock depuis Excel, relance tes clients impayés en un tap sur WhatsApp — deux trucs que Boutik fait pour toi.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed, light UI-accurate SFX
- Music: bundled `happy-beats-business-moves` track (different volume from the first brag for variety), low and steady — this is explainer content, not a hype reel, so keep the bed unobtrusive throughout
- Music treatment: soft fade-in under the hook, flat low level through both demos, gentle fade under the outro — no big swells, no climax transition this time (nothing here needs a "launch" moment)
- Music cue guidance: none required — this video prioritizes clarity over rhythm; only the row-correction and message-reveal beats get their own SFX, not beat-locked
- Audio-reactive treatment: none
- SFX posture: sparse — one soft tick per discrete UI action (file chosen, row corrected, save tap, relance tap), one distinct "message sent" tone for the WhatsApp reveal
- Audio-coupled moments: file-picked tick, save-button tap, relance-button tap, message-bubble arrival tone
- Restraint rule: no music swell anywhere in this video — it should read as calm and clear, not exciting

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 2.2s
Brand green background. Text slams in: "2 trucs qui font gagner du temps."
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: quiet anticipation
Audio-coupled idea: none
Music: soft bed fades in
Transition mood: clean crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Import: pick file — 3.5s
Import page real UI: "Importer mon stock" header, dashed upload box "Choisir un fichier Excel (.xlsx)", tap simulated, box shows "Lecture du fichier…" briefly.
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulated tap on the upload box, brief loading state
Audio intent: tactile, quick
Audio-coupled idea: soft tick when the file is "chosen"
Transition mood: clean crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Import: preview & save — 4s
Preview table appears: 4 rows (name/price/stock/category), one row highlighted amber as "à corriger", then clears to valid; "Enregistrer 4 produit(s)" button taps; cut-in confirmation.
Sequential/interaction: yes — rows arrive one by one, correction highlight settles, button taps
Audio intent: build toward the save action
Audio-coupled idea: row-arrival ticks (sparse, not one per row), save-tap confirm
Transition mood: crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Bridge: name feature 2 — 1.6s
Brand green card: "Relancer un client qui n'a pas payé."
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: clean beat, topic change
Audio-coupled idea: none
Transition mood: crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Relance: tap and message reveal — 5.2s
Relances page real UI: client card "Aïcha Koné — impayé depuis 6 jours", amount, tap "Relancer" button, a WhatsApp-green chat bubble slides up containing the real relance message text.
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulated tap on Relancer, chat bubble reveal with real message copy
Audio intent: payoff, satisfying
Audio-coupled idea: relance-tap tick, message-bubble arrival tone
Transition mood: crossfade → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — Outro — 2.5s
Logo-full lockup on dark card + line: "Importe. Vends. Relance. Tout depuis ton téléphone."
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: quiet landing
Audio-coupled idea: none
Music: fades out
Transition mood: crossfade (end)
